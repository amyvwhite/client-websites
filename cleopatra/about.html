About

Cleopatra Boudreau is a New York-based multihyphenate working across film and theatre. A graduate of Concordia University's Theatre Performance program, she gravitates toward bold, physically expressive work that finds the funny and vulnerable in human relationships. She's particularly interested in queer identity, intimacy, friendship, and people behaving badly while feeling completely justified in doing so.

Her credits span stage and screen, including Mia M.I.A. at La MaMa Experimental Theatre Club, Loneliness Was a Pandemic with TheaterLab, and Psychopathia Paschalis at The Chain Theatre. On screen, she has appeared in The Bold Type (Freeform) and Bellevue (CBC), and the upcoming feature The Allegory (Xavier Films).

Beyond performing, Cleopatra writes, produces, and develops original film and theatre through Other Half Productions. Her work tends to be emotionally honest without taking itself too seriously.
