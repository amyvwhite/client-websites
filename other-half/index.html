about

other half productions is an independent production company founded by actor, writer, and producer cleopatra boudreau.

we make films about connection, identity, ambition, desire, and the wonderfully complicated business of being a person.

our work centers queer friendships and the kinds of relationships that make you think, "i'd probably help you hide a dead body."

we prioritize working with women and queer creatives, especially when their lived experience connects to the stories we're telling. we believe the people represented on screen should have a voice behind the camera, too. and honestly, we just like working with our community.

from experimental films about discovery to stories about chasing a dream without losing the people you love along the way, we're interested in the messy, surprising ways people find themselves and each other.

basically, we make films about finding your people.

even if your people are terrible influences.
